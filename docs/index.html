EGOCENTRIC MANIPULATION · HUMAN SIGNALS

Human activity, and the signals behind it.

HumanCOIN connects egocentric multi-view observations with human signals, including gaze, surface EMG, EEG and glove-based finger kinematics. Three tiers—visual, motor and cognitive intention—share a unified spatiotemporal reference, bringing scene coverage and signal depth together for embodied manipulation research.
